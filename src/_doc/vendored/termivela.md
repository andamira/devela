[Terminus Font](https://terminus-font.sourceforge.net/) 4.49.1
is vendored in converted form as the Termivela bitmap-font family.

The initial version includes regular and bold 8×16 strikes,
with the following modifications:

- Adopted the name "Termivela" to distinguish the derivative from the Reserved Font Name "Terminus Font".
- Converted the Unicode scalar mappings, fixed metrics and monochrome glyph bitmaps from BDF to DVBF.
- Made no intentional changes to glyph shapes or font metrics.
