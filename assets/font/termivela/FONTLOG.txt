Termivela FONTLOG

Version 0.1.0 — 2026-07-31

- Created the Termivela family from the regular and bold 8×16 BDF faces of Terminus Font 4.49.1.
- Converted the Unicode scalar mappings, fixed metrics and monochrome glyph bitmaps to DVBF 0.1.0.
- Adopted the name "Termivela" to distinguish the derivative from the Reserved Font Name "Terminus Font".
- Made no intentional changes to glyph shapes or font metrics.

Future modifications

Future versions may add custom glyphs, amend existing glyphs, extend character coverage, or introduce additional bitmap strikes. Such changes will be recorded here.
